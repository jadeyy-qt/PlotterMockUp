FAQ

Billing questions

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade anytime from your account settings. Your stories are never deleted — if you go over the Freemium limit, extra stories are simply unpublished until you upgrade.

Do my students need to pay?

Never. Students play stories for free through a share link, on any plan.

How do marketplace payouts work?

Creators receive 70% of each sale. Earnings are paid out monthly once your balance reaches the minimum payout amount.

Is there a discount for schools?

The School plan covers your whole school for $150 per year. Contact us if you'd like a quote for a district.
