FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Plotter really free?

Yes. Plotter is free for individual educators throughout the open beta, with no credit card required.

Do my students need accounts?

No. When you publish a story you get a share link — students open it in any browser and start playing right away.

Do I need any design or coding skills?

None at all. If you can build a slideshow, you can build a Plotter story. Characters, backgrounds, and templates are included.

Can I use my own images?

Custom backgrounds and characters are on our roadmap. For now, the built-in library covers classrooms, school settings, and historical scenes.

What grade levels is Plotter for?

Templates currently span upper elementary through high school, and every story can be adapted to the level of your class.

Ready to bring your curriculum to life?

Build your first story today. It's free for individual teachers — and your students will thank you.
